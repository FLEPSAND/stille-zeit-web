Privacy Policy

As of: 5 November 2026 · Version 1.2

This policy describes which data the app “Quiet Time” processes. This English version is a translation for your understanding; the binding version is the German one. The policy is deliberately concrete: for every function that lets data leave the device, it states here where the data goes and why.

In the following, we refer to ourselves as “we”.

2. At a glance

The app works without an account with us. We operate no server that holds your content, and we receive no data from you.

Your prayer requests, answered prayers, sessions and settings stay on your device. Data is transferred outward only at your request: the iCloud sync and the retrieval of the Open Doors prayer requests. Purchases are handled by Apple.

We use no analytics, tracking or advertising services. No profiling takes place.

3. Data on your device

None of your entries leave the device on their own. The following are stored locally (technically via “shared_preferences” in the app storage):

  • Prayer requests with title, notes, category and status
  • Answered prayers with date and note
  • Session history with duration, date and journal notes
  • Reading plans and reading progress
  • Highlighted Bible passages with note
  • Profile, prayer times and settings
  • How often the app was opened and when it last asked for a rating, so that it does not ask too often

This data is not additionally encrypted; it lies in the protected app area of your device. If you delete the app, it is deleted along with it.

Legal basis: the processing takes place exclusively on your device; no data is transferred to us.

4. Device sync via iCloud (optional)

If you switch on sync in the settings, your prayer requests, answered prayers, sessions, categories, prayer times, reading plans and highlighted Bible passages are written to your personal iCloud key-value store so that they are available on your other devices. Profile and appearance are not synced.

The data lies in your own iCloud account with Apple. We have no access to it. Apple is responsible for the storage in iCloud; Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Sync is off by default and can be switched off again at any time. Switching it off deletes nothing.

Legal basis: consent by switching on sync (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), revocable at any time.

5. Purchases

The Basic version is permanently free. Optionally, “Quiet Time Pro” can be purchased as a subscription or as a one-time purchase.

Payment is handled exclusively by Apple via your Apple ID. We receive no payment data, no address and no name. The app merely asks Apple whether a valid purchase exists and stores the result, including the expiry date, locally on the device.

Apple is responsible for payment processing; Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

6. Prayer requests from Open Doors

The app can retrieve the publicly provided prayer requests from Open Doors Germany (opendoors.de) on a daily basis. As with any visit to a website, your IP address is transferred to Open Doors or their hoster. We transfer no data about you in the process.

The retrieved texts are cached locally so that retrieval takes place at most once a day.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in providing the function (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

7. Church channel for sermons (optional)

If you enter the address of a church channel in the settings, the app retrieves a file there at most twice a day. It contains your church’s sermons and, if the church uses it, general prayer requests without personal reference.

As with any visit to a website, your IP address is transferred to the provider of that address, i.e. usually to your church or their hoster. We transfer no data about you, and no account is created.

Without an entered channel, no retrieval takes place. The function is off by default.

The church that provides the channel is responsible for its content. Notes that you write yourself about a sermon stay on your device and are not overwritten by a retrieval.

Legal basis: consent by entering the address (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), revocable at any time by clearing the field.

8. Reminders

Reminders for your prayer times are scheduled exclusively locally on the device. There is no push service and no server that learns about them. You grant the permission for this in iOS and can withdraw it there at any time.

9. App lock (optional)

If you turn on the app lock (a Quiet Time Pro feature), you allow the app to access iOS Screen Time. It uses Apple’s “Family Controls”, “Managed Settings” and “Device Activity” interfaces for this. You choose the apps, categories and websites in a picker shown by Apple itself. The app only receives identifiers (tokens) from which it cannot tell which apps they are.

These identifiers, whether the lock is on, when it was last unlocked, whether it locks once a day or at your prayer times, the times and weekdays of the prayer times you have switched on, whether Pro is active and which language the app uses are kept in a storage area shared by the app and its two extensions on your device (App Group). None of it is transferred, neither to us nor via iCloud sync. The app does not read usage times or any other information about how you use your apps.

You can withdraw access at any time in the iOS settings under Screen Time; the app can then no longer lock anything. If you delete the app, the stored selection is deleted as well.

10. Year in review as PDF

The year in review is created on your device. Whether and with whom you share the file is up to you alone, via iOS’s share function. Nothing is transferred to us.

11. Retention

Since we store no data from you, there are no retention periods with us. Local data remains until you remove it in the app or by deleting the app. Data stored in iCloud you remove via your device’s iCloud settings.

12. Your rights

You have the rights of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Since we process no personal data from you, these rights are largely moot with respect to us — for data in iCloud, contact Apple.

You can complain to a data protection supervisory authority at any time. For us, the competent authority is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of Baden-Württemberg.

13. Children and young people

The app is aimed at adults and young people. It collects no data on our side and contains no advertising.

14. Changes

We adapt this policy as soon as the data processing in the app changes. The current version can be viewed in the app under “Me → About the app → Privacy”.

15. Contact

If you have questions about data protection, you can reach us at andreas.fleps@proton.me.
